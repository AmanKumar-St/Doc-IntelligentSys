Apex Global Enterprise - Employee Handbook 2026

# 1. Paid Time Off and Leave Policy

All full-time employees are allocated 22 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accumulates at a rate of 1.83 days per month worked. Employees may carry forward a maximum of 5 unused leave days into the next calendar year, which must be utilized before March 31st.

Sick leave is granted at 12 days per year with full pay. For medical leaves extending beyond 3 consecutive working days, a formal medical practitioner certificate is required upon return.

# 2. Remote Work & Equipment Reimbursement

Apex Global supports a flexible hybrid work model. Employees in eligible technical and operational roles may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager consent.

# 3. Core Working Hours & Overtime

Standard working hours are 40 hours per week. Core collaboration hours across all timezones are established between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM local office time.
